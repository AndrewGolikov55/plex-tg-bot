Plex apps iOS The official Plex app on the App Store has a free 5-minute playback limit per video. Two ways around it: • Plex Pass — buy once, unlocks all features. • app.plex.tv in Safari — no limit, browser-only. Android Two options: • Official Plex on Google Play — same 5-minute limit unless you have Plex Pass. • A community Plex fork — drop-in replacement without the playback limit. (Pick one and link from your own apps.html.) Smart TVs LG (webOS), Samsung (Tizen), Apple TV, Roku, Fire TV — all have official Plex apps in their stores. Desktop • macOS / Windows: plex.tv/media-server-downloads • Browser: app.plex.tv Trouble signing in After accepting the invite, sign in with the same account. If you see "no servers available", wait a few minutes and refresh.
