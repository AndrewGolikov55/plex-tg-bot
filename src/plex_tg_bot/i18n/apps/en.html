Plex apps iOS / iPadOS The official Plex app on the App Store has a free 5-minute playback limit per video. Options: • app.plex.tv in Safari — free, no limit, browser-only. • Plex Pass — paid subscription, unlocks the full mobile app. • Infuse — paid third-party player, polished UI, plays from Plex servers without the 5-minute limit. Android • Official Plex on Google Play — same 5-minute limit unless you have Plex Pass. • app.plex.tv in Chrome / Firefox — free, no limit. • Kodi + PlexKodiConnect — free, full-featured, also works on Android TV / Fire TV. • Community Plex forks (sideloaded APKs) drop the playback limit; search current ones at your own discretion. Apple TV • Official Plex app on tvOS — full features, no playback limit. • Infuse — paid alternative with a nicer interface. Smart TVs and streaming sticks Official Plex apps are in every major store: • Samsung Tizen — Samsung Apps / Tizen Store • LG webOS — LG Content Store • Android TV / Google TV / Chromecast with Google TV — Google Play • Roku — Channel Store • Amazon Fire TV / Fire TV Stick — Amazon Appstore • Apple TV — App Store (tvOS) Older TV models occasionally lag behind on app updates. If playback breaks, sideload Kodi with PlexKodiConnect. Desktop • Native: Plex apps for macOS / Windows / Linux. • Browser: app.plex.tv — works in any modern browser. Trouble signing in After accepting the invite, sign in with the same account you gave to the admin. If you see "no servers available", wait a few minutes and refresh.
