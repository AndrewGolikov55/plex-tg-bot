# Plex apps

## iOS

The official **Plex** app on the App Store has a free 5-minute playback limit per video. Two ways around it:

- **Plex Pass** — buy once, unlocks all features. https://www.plex.tv/plex-pass/
- **Browser** — open https://app.plex.tv on Safari. No limit.

## Smart TVs

LG (webOS), Samsung (Tizen), Apple TV, Roku, and Fire TV all have official Plex apps available in their stores.

## Desktop

- macOS / Windows: https://www.plex.tv/media-server-downloads/#plex-app
- Browser: https://app.plex.tv

## Trouble signing in

After accepting the email invite, sign in with the same account. If you see "no servers available", wait a few minutes and refresh.
